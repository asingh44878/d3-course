Introduction

Structural elements are the building blocks of a web page. They define the structure and layout of the content, making it easier for users to navigate and understand the information presented.

Common Structural Elements

  • Header: Contains introductory content or navigational links.
  • Main: Represents the dominant content of the document.
  • Footer: Contains information about the author, copyright, or related documents.
  • Section: Represents a standalone section of content.
  • Article: Represents a self-contained composition in a document.
